### 1. Ambiguous Tier 2 / Tier 3 Macro Split Reintroduces Dual-Owner Interference
- **Document:** `docs/REFOCUS.md` §3 Sol. 3–4 (lines 195–201, 241), §4.1 (line 330), §4.3 Phase 1 (line 379).
- **Source:** `gary/agent/macro.py:217–228, 384–416, 479, 548–566, 625–637`; `train/macro.py:201–220`; `gary/agent/manifests.py:261, 1224, 1393–1396`; `runs/league/HS284a/Attitude SE 2.1/gt1dev_seed4004.records.jsonl:79, 120`; `docs/PROGRESS.md:1367–1370, 1712–1795` `[read in source]`.
- **Critique:** `REFOCUS.md:241` leaves two mutually incompatible designs under an *"or"*: conditioning `MacroNet` on `StrategyState` *vs.* running a symbolic milestone scheduler alongside `MacroNet` for continuous unit/supply/expansion flow. If a symbolic scheduler emits milestones alongside an unconditioned `MacroNet`, it recreates Gary's existing conflict (`answers.py` + `parkmany`): `_kept_cost` (`macro.py:625–637`) only reserves already-queued items so continuous production steals minerals from upcoming card milestones, forced builds push `decisions so far` out-of-distribution (`PROGRESS.md:1367–1370`), and lingering decoder crutches (`under-half` at `macro.py:479`, still active in `gt1dev_seed4004.records.jsonl:79, 120`, plus `odds_mass`/`expand_mass`/`answers` in `gt1dev`) silently override intent `[inferred]`.
- **Fix:** Apply strict **preemptive shielding** (**Alshiekh et al., 2018**, §7 ref): Tier 2 holds a formal mineral/gas reservation budget (`reserved_for_milestones`) and hard legal action masks, while `MacroNet` is retrained/conditioned on *remaining card deltas and unreserved income*, removing `odds_mass`, `expand_mass`, `under-half`, `answers.py`, and `rax_by` from `gt_ns`.

### 2. Squad Options Discard Spatial Precision & Repeat `#53`'s Flawed Local RL Reward
- **Document:** `docs/REFOCUS.md` §3 Sol. 3–4 (lines 203–210, 240–246), §4.3 Phase 2 (lines 384–391), §6.1 H2–H3 (lines 452–453).
- **Source:** `docs/PROGRESS.md:989–1014, 1145–1161, 1346–1358` (`#53`), `2160–2170`; `docs/SELF_PLAY.md:485–493`; `gary/agent/army.py:630–690`; `gary/agent/intents.py:27–30` `[read in source]`.
- **Critique:**
  1. *Repeating `#53`:* `REFOCUS.md:245` proposes 15–45 s leaf RL with purely local option rewards (`DISENGAGE` = survival + distance; `ENGAGE` = local trade; `HARASS` = worker damage/loss). `PROGRESS.md:989–1014, 1346–1358` (`#53`) **already falsified** local trade rewards in Gary: `rl2` (10 s) and `rl3` (20 s) beat `rl1` by `+67` and `+71` on 79 held-out 120 s scenarios while failing 96-game gates (`-0.036`, `-0.013`) because local rewards ignore macro attrition and opportunity cost `[read in source]`.
  2. *Missing spatial geometry & rally absorption:* Collapsing out-of-contact movement to 8–9 discrete region options loses `ArmyNet`'s `16x16` spatial targeting (needed for flanking angles, concave staging, and stray rally units—`units alone` is `0.40` vs pros `0.20`, `PROGRESS.md:2160–2170`), while hand-authored thresholds (`>=80%` or `15 s`, `REFOCUS.md:208`) risk threshold oscillation `[inferred]`.
- **Literature Fixes:**
  - *Value-shaped finite episodes or offline SMDP RL:* Do not run unshaped local RL. Add **Potential-Based Reward Shaping** (`Phi(s) = ValueNet(s)`, **Ng, Harada, & Russell, 1999**) or full SMDP termination continuation values (**Sutton, Precup, & Singh, 1999**, §7 ref) to credit boundary states, or replace online sub-episode PG with offline behavior-regularized RL (**IQL**, **Kostrikov, Nair, & Levine, 2021**; **CQL**, **Kumar et al., 2020**; or offline primitive abstraction like **OPAL**, **Ajay et al., 2020**) so human-hand stochasticity does not swamp policy gradients. (Note **Levy et al., 2019**, §7 ref, relies on subgoal state reachability rather than adversarial net-value trade-offs.)
  - *Deliberation-costed persistence + baseline spatial heads:* Penalize mid-flight option switching with an explicit **deliberation cost** (**Harb et al., 2018**, refining **Bacon, Harb, & Precup, 2017**, §7 ref) tied to camera/selection cost (`Q3`). Make `ArmyNet`'s `16x16` heatmap a baseline spatial target proposal inside `TIMING_PUSH`/`CONTAIN`/`STAGE` from day one of Phase 2 (not only an `H2` fallback) alongside a `REINFORCE_SQUAD` lease rule; strong sample-efficient SC2 agents (**SCC**, **Wang et al., 2020**; **TStarBot-X**, **Han et al., 2020**) retain spatial coordinate heads beneath strategic selectors.

### 3. Soft Concept Leakage, Independent Marginals, and Confounded CRM Weaken Sol. 2 & 3
- **Document:** `docs/REFOCUS.md` §3 Sol. 2–3 (lines 166–188, 213–224), §5.2 Q2 & Q4 (lines 423, 425).
- **Source:** `gary/policy/belief.py:1–100`; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:1115–1123` `[read in source]`.
- **Critique:**
  1. *Independent marginal beliefs under fog & soft CBM leakage:* Predicting `P(early_pool)`, `P(spire_muta)`, `P(lurker_tech)`, and `P(ling_hit)` independently discards mutually exclusive Brood War tech/gas structure, while feeding continuous `P(concept)` into `ebm_ga2m`/`concept_mlp` recreates the exact **soft-probability leakage** (**Mahinpei et al., 2021**; **Havasi, Parbhoo, & Doshi-Velez, 2022**, §7 refs) and **intervention failure** (**Shin et al., 2023**) that `REFOCUS.md` worries about (`Q2`). Note `gary/policy/belief.py:44–99` *already* computes multi-window (`30/90/180 s/ever`) fog memory and predicts enemy unit counts, workers, halls, and supply percentiles (`[read in source]`), so Tier 1 should extend `BeliefModel` rather than duplicate it `[inferred]`.
  2. *Unobserved confounding violates CRM assumptions:* `REFOCUS.md:216, 223, 425` leans on **Swaminathan & Joachims (2015)** (§7 ref) to learn `Provider C` from logged pro replays. Counterfactual Risk Minimization assumes **known logging propensities / unconfoundedness**. Pro choices depend heavily on unlogged latent state (hidden worker pressure, micro readiness, player APM state), leaving observational IPS biased `[inferred]`.
- **Literature Fixes:** Replace independent tech flags with a **joint Bayesian / HMM plan-recognition posterior** (`P(enemy z | timeline observations)`, **Synnaeve & Bessière, 2011**; **Dereszynski et al., 2011**; **Weber & Mateas, 2009**) audited for **reasoning shortcuts** (**Marconato et al., 2023**, §7 ref) and relational concept pruning (**SCoBots**, **Delfosse et al., 2024**, §7 ref); when testing continuous bottlenecks, use **CEM / IntCEM** (**Espinosa Zarlenga et al., 2022, 2023**) for intervention-aware training. For confounded pro logs (`Q4`), use **Confounding-Robust Policy Improvement** bounds (**Kallus & Zhou, 2018**) and **POMDP Off-Policy Evaluation** (**Tennenholtz, Mannor, & Shalit, 2020**), reserving CRM strictly for randomized `eval/pairs.py` branches where propensity is truly known.
