## Is a major refocus necessary, or would the current direction get there?

A targeted refocus of the decision trunk is necessary; the six-solution big-bang refocus as scoped is not proven necessary. The distinction matters, and the repo itself draws it.

For G1 (plays like a real opponent) the current direction demonstrably gets there eventually, slowly. Situation-level bug-fixing plus retraining has repeatedly closed severalfold gaps in weeks: tanks sieged 0% to 61.3% against a pro's 54.3% once the TvZ role labels named siege ([docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2015), read in source), the calm layer matched the pros' short-order rate 1.43 against 1.58 a minute ([docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2228), read in source), and transfers, placement and cluster fixes show the same pattern. Nothing in that record says marginal behavior cloning (BC) plus the judge/pipe programme in [TODO.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/TODO.md:33) (Direction c: upstream army positioning and finishing) stalls permanently on G1. It is slow and whack-a-mole, but it is converging on the fidelity measures it targets.

For G3 (steerable: "practice vs. 2-hatch muta 20 times") and G5 (faithfully explainable for learners) the current direction cannot get there at any amount of bug-fixing, for a structural reason the plan identifies correctly. The goals are in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:55): G3 requires a strategy spec followed reliably, G5 a trace of intent, belief and alternatives. In play today there is no belief over the enemy's plan at all: the opponent model is listed as "no inference of the enemy's *z*" ([docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:30), read in source), the army belief model is "not used in play by default" ([docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:59), read in source) and is only an optional army option (`belief` in [gary/agent/manifests.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/manifests.py:790), read in source), and the only strategy state is a style integer rolled at 0:00 ([gary/agent/manifests.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/manifests.py:470), `rolled_style`, read in source). A narrator over records that contain no plan and no enemy-plan belief can only ever produce the "faithful nonsense" of the plan's §1 table, however many decoder bugs are fixed. That is an architectural absence, not a bug count.

Two qualifications keep this from endorsing the plan wholesale. First, the missing trunk is not a new invention: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:126) Principle 4, §6.1 `StrategySpec`, §6.8 Strategy Selector and §7.5 Opponent Model already designed exactly a symbolic trunk learned by counting, and the selector already implements its Beta-posterior core ([gary/agent/selector.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/selector.py:62), read in source). The refocus is a return to the original architecture after the CONTROL.md layer stack displaced it, so it can be built incrementally on the existing selector/posture/macro seams rather than as a parallel `gt_ns` universe. Second, the current TODO direction is already walking toward the trunk: Direction c puts "the upstream decisions ahead of the remaining abilities: the army's positioning and engagements, and finishing games" next ([TODO.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/TODO.md:33), read in source). The necessary move is to accelerate that walk with one Phase-1-style slice (opponent-plan belief + recipe card + counted branches + templated answers, gated live), not to pause all other matchups, delete working layers, and rebuild six solutions at once before any slice is falsified.

Verdict: refocus the trunk, yes; adopt the plan's scope and sequencing as written, no. Findings below, most important first.

## Findings

### 1. The "post-posed override kills RL" diagnosis misdescribes the lease system it cites
Document section: Executive Summary point 1 and §2.1. Source: [gary/agent/intents.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/intents.py:27) — read in source.
`priority()` gives rules priority 3, fight 2, army 1, and with `"leases": "enforced"` a lower-priority decision simply leaves out units a higher-priority source still holds ([gary/agent/intents.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/intents.py:54), read in source). That is visible, traced (`lease_conflict`, same file line 73) unit sequestration, not a stealthy post-hoc click overwrite. The shielding citation itself is accurate — Alshiekh et al., *Safe Reinforcement Learning via Shielding*, AAAI 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08611, opened in this run, does define preemptive versus post-posed shielding as the plan says — but Alshiekh's post-posed shield corrects an action the learner chose, whereas Gary's rules never let the learner's intent reach those units at all. The RL harm is real but its mechanism is narrower action ownership and non-stationarity, not broken on-policy attribution in Alshiekh's sense. The fix the plan wants (exclusive leases at option boundaries) is largely a re-labelling of the existing lease machinery, which weakens the claim that a new Tier 2/3 contract layer is required to obtain it.

### 3. The RL ledger (§2.3) proves less than it claims, because its strongest row is a deleted, whole-army contrast
Document section: §2.3. Source: [docs/SELF_PLAY.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/SELF_PLAY.md:488) — read in source.
The ledger's load-bearing positive result, "whole-army coherent intent, sd 650 vs chaos 210 (3x SNR)", comes from `eval/army_effect.py`, which SELF_PLAY itself notes is "since deleted" (same line), and the contrast was the *whole army attacking versus holding* — far larger than any single option choice a trunk would actually score. SELF_PLAY says the same at line 336 in [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:193) terms: the model's own alternatives are much smaller than that contrast. Conversely the negative rows are real and well-sourced (macro branches 50.0% held-out in [docs/CONTROL.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/CONTROL.md:240), read in source; commit pairs at chance, held-out 0.46 on 1,004 pairs in [docs/SELF_PLAY.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/SELF_PLAY.md:513), read in source). The honest inference is "signal grows with temporal extent and decision size", not "options at 30-90 s have 3x SNR": a `TIMING_PUSH` versus `CONTAIN` choice is intermediate in size and its SNR is unmeasured. Phase 2's acceptance gate should require measuring option-level SNR first, which the plan's H2/H3 do not.

### 4. The selector's success is evidence the trunk already exists in embryo, not that it must be rebuilt
Document section: §2.3 (selector row) and Solution 3. Source: [gary/agent/selector.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/selector.py:47) — read in source.
The one clean strategic-level learning win the ledger cites (losses 57 to 30 of 333, [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:39), read in source) is the §6.8 counted-table selector: Beta posteriors per (map, option) with hierarchical counts already in code (`counts`/`choose`, same file lines 47-68). Solution 3's Provider A (counted tables with shrinkage) and the factored `StrategyState` are extensions of this component and of `posture.py`, not replacements for a vacuum. Framing §2.2 as "nothing symbolic was built at the trunk" is therefore overstated: what is missing is specifically (a) conditioning those counts on an enemy-plan belief and scouted context, and (b) persisting a plan state between decisions. Both are additive changes to existing providers behind the existing manifest seam ([gary/agent/manifests.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/manifests.py:1), "one provider per layer", read in source). This cuts the Phase 0/1 cost substantially and removes the argument for a clean-slate `gt_ns` line.

### 5. The CalmNet "proof" that marginal matching produces incoherence misattributes the cause its own source found
Document section: §2.2 point 2. Source: [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2237) — read in source.
The plan quotes the direction head's log-loss 2.67 against uniform 2.77 as "nearly random Brownian motion". PROGRESS's follow-up read (same entry, lines 2237-2249) found the scattering was not primarily direction ignorance: Gary's calm orders went to a median 2 units, 8.8 tiles from any other fighter, against the pros' 7 units at 2.2 tiles, and 89% of its one-unit orders were to units already alone. The far head, on the same marginal-matching design, learned materially more (direction 2.495 against uniform 2.773, [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2255), read in source). So the measured failure is *which bunches get orders* (a grouping/cluster-membership problem, since fixed for commit clusters at [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2275)), not a proof that per-tick marginal BC is incoherent in principle. Solution 2/4's conclusion that out-of-contact positioning must move wholesale into symbolic squad options (and §4.1's DELETE of `calm.py`, a layer folded into gt1dev at gt0.46dev for measured standing gains) rests on a superseded diagnosis. Keep calm as a leaf provider under an option lease; do not delete it on this evidence.

### 6. The model inventory in §2.2 inflates the fragmentation it criticises
Document section: §2.2. Source: [gary/policy/calm.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/policy/calm.py:5) — read in source.
The "~10 independent models" list double-counts: `CalmNet`/`CalmFarNet` are not separate networks but a role network (`RoleNet`, [gary/policy/roles.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/policy/roles.py:224), read in source) reused over calm snapshots ("The calm model is a role network", `calm.py` line 5), and the listed `EventModel` is the event head inside [gary/policy/modes.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/policy/modes.py:177) (`ModeModel` + `EVENTS`, wired as `event_model=True` in [gary/agent/manifests.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/manifests.py:750), read in source), not a tenth free-standing model. The real count of distinct learned providers in a dev build is smaller, and several already share feature code (`fight.py rows_at shared`, [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2185)). Fragmentation and tug-of-war are real (Finding 8), but the plan should argue from the shared-code reality, because it changes Solution 4: conditioning `RoleNet` on an option is conditioning the same network that already serves calm and fight, which is cheaper than the plan implies — and also riskier, since one network's retraining then moves three behaviours at once.

### 7. Strict concept bottleneck (Solution 2) imports a static-classification result into a temporal, adversarial setting without a temporal concept semantics
Document section: Solution 2. Source: none in repo (literature finding) — inferred from the cited papers.
Koh et al., *Concept Bottleneck Models*, ICML 2020, https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.04612 — opened in this run — intervenes on concepts of a single input (an x-ray, a bird photo) to predict a single label. Gary's decisive concepts are temporal and relational over windows (`timing_window_active`, threat ETAs, "Lair finishing with no Spire seen yet"), and the plan's own §5 admits no cited system operates at this scale. Delfosse et al., *Interpretable Concept Bottlenecks to Align Reinforcement Learning Agents* (SCoBots), NeurIPS 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05821 — opened in this run; the plan's author list is correct — does add relational concepts, but on fully observable Atari with per-step policies, not on calibrated beliefs consumed by a separate symbolic scorer. Two specific gaps the plan must answer before Phase 1: (a) what a concept *intervention* means when the concept is a probability over a hidden plan inferred from fog — flipping `P(2h_muta)` in a record does not change what was scouted, so Koh-style intervention accuracy does not transfer directly; (b) leakage: Havasi, Parbhoo & Doshi-Velez, *Addressing Leakage in Concept Bottleneck Models*, NeurIPS 2022, https://openreview.net/forum?id=tglniD_fn9 (searched, not opened) shows soft probabilities themselves leak, and a learned `concept_mlp`/EBM scorer over 30-60 soft concept probabilities is exactly the leaky configuration. The plan names this risk but its mitigation (hard supervision from resim ground truth) addresses concept *predictor* semantics, not downstream scorer leakage. Require a hard-concept (thresholded/sampled) scorer variant in the Phase 1 bake-off, not only in Phase 3.

### 8. The army tug-of-war is real, but its measured cause is re-decision frequency, not missing symbolic options
Document section: §2.2 point 3 and Solution 3 part 2. Source: [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2311) — read in source.
The plan attributes cancelled retreats and redirected orders to four uncoordinated systems. PROGRESS's pipe read found commit orders are redirected within 30 s at 63-73%, "but that is the pros' own pattern" (pros 0.79, gt1dev 0.70 on the same measure), i.e. re-decision every 5 s (`COMMIT_TICK`, [gary/agent/army.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/army.py:50), read in source) is itself pro-like. What is *not* pro-like is the retreat *distance* (where-head points a median ~3 tiles, [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2370)) and modes leaving under army moves. Persistent options with termination predicates would fix the distance/hold problem only if the leaf's where-head changes; an option that holds a 3-tile retreat point for 15 s holds the wrong point longer. The cheaper, literature-standard framing is Sutton, Precup & Singh, *Between MDPs and semi-MDPs*, Artificial Intelligence 1999 (searched, not opened; options as initiation/policy/termination triples over semi-MDPs): Gary's commitments already are options in everything but persistent termination, since the army data's hazard of reversal was measured and matched ([docs/SELF_PLAY.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/SELF_PLAY.md:490), read in source). Add explicit termination/hold to the existing commitment first and measure cancelled-retreat rate; build the 9-option squad layer only if that fails.

### 9. EBM/GA2M branch scoring (Solution 3, Provider B) cannot express the conjunctions Brood War timing decisions turn on
Document section: Solution 3 part 3. Source: none in repo — inferred from the cited method.
Nori et al., *InterpretML: A Unified Framework for Machine Learning Interpretability*, 2019, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09223 (searched, not opened) introduces Explainable Boosting Machines as GAMs plus *pairwise* interactions (GA2M) with exact per-feature attributions. A timing push decision is characteristically a three-way conjunction (stim done AND +1 done AND enemy third just taken AND regroup complete); pairwise additive terms must approximate it through correlated pairs, and the "exact faithful contribution" reading then misattributes a genuinely conjunctive cause to its pairs — the same faithfulness failure the plan charges against `attribution.py` zeroing ([gary/policy/attribution.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/policy/attribution.py:1), read in source: groups zeroed in turn). Either allow depth-limited interaction trees as a Provider B variant and drop the exact-attribution claim for it, or restrict EBM to genuinely additive branch families (turret counts per belief, expansion timing) and say so. As written, Provider B promises both expressiveness and exact attribution; GA2M delivers the second by sacrificing the first.

### 10. Counted pro tables are not bandit data, and the cited offline-bandit method needs propensities Gary does not have
Document section: Solution 3 part 3 and §5.2 Q4. Source: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:483) — read in source.
Swaminathan & Joachims, *Batch Learning from Logged Bandit Feedback through Counterfactual Risk Minimization*, JMLR 2015 (searched, not opened) corrects logged bandit feedback where the logging policy's action propensities are known. In pro replays the "logging policy" is a human's unobserved decision process: the propensity of a pro choosing 2-hatch muta given his scout information is not recorded and not estimable from the replay without the very opponent-model assumptions under test. ARCHITECTURE already flags this ("win counts for cells", with confounding noted in the plan's own Q4, and §6.8's table built from *Gary's own games* where propensities — the selector's Thompson draws — actually are known, [gary/agent/selector.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/agent/selector.py:68), read in source). The plan should therefore source causal branch scores from Gary-vs-Gary interventions (where the branching engine gives true propensities by construction) and use pro tables only as priors with printed `n`, never propensity-weighted as if they were logged bandits. The §5.3 counterfactual-branch verification idea is the right instrument for this and deserves promotion from "candidate novel direction" to the main estimator.

### 11. The counterfactual faithfulness test (Solution 6) will drown in the chaos it elsewhere cites, unless it pre-registers repeats
Document section: Solution 6. Source: [docs/CONTROL.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/CONTROL.md:276) — read in source.
The plan proposes verifying an explanation by flipping the cited concept at frame `t` in `eval/pairs.py` and checking the branch changes. CONTROL measured that over 3 minutes chaos spreads a pair by sd 512 while a single macro decision's real effect is sd 130, and replays "barely agree on which decision was better (+0.14, interval -0.18 to +0.35)" without `--repeats` (6 replays per arm). A single flipped-concept branch therefore cannot distinguish "concept was decisive" from hand-noise, and an 80% counterfactual-consistency kill criterion (H1) measured on single branches is uninterpretable. The test is sound only at the *decision* level (does the trunk scorer, re-queried on the intervened concept state, change its argmax — deterministic and cheap) plus a repeated-branch outcome check for the subset of claims about outcomes. Split H1 accordingly; the decision-level test needs no game branches at all and can run in Phase 1 week 1.

### 12. Goal-conditioned leaves (Solution 4) re-create the CalmNet unobserved-intent problem at the labelling step
Document section: Solution 4. Source: [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2200) — inferred from source.
Conditioning `RoleNet`/`MacroNet` on the active option requires pro replays labelled with the option the pro was "in". That intent is unobserved for exactly the reason §2.2 gives against marginal BC: the pros' short-order direction was unpredictable from every candidate the project tried (cosines 0.00-0.19 against bunch centre, enemy side, last far order). Heuristic option labellers (distance-to-enemy thresholds for `CONTAIN` vs `TIMING_PUSH`) will encode the labeller's definitions, and the conditioned model will learn to execute *the labeller's* options — a reasoning-shortcut risk of the kind Marconato et al., *Not All Neuro-Symbolic Concepts Are Created Equal*, NeurIPS 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19951 (searched, not opened) formalise, which the plan cites for concepts but not for option labels. Levy et al., *Learning Multi-Level Hierarchies with Hindsight* (HAC), ICLR 2019, https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.00948 (searched, not opened) is cited for alternating training, but its actual remedy — hindsight relabelling of achieved goals — suggests the concrete fix: label pro segments by the goal *achieved* (where the army ended, what it killed) rather than the goal presumed intended, and report label agreement between two independent labellers as a Phase 2 entry gate.

### 13. The residual concept-invention loop (Solution 5) starts from a ValueNet that its own evaluation says cannot foresee
Document section: Solution 5 part 2. Source: [docs/CONTROL.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/CONTROL.md:251) — read in source.
The loop clusters states where `V_unconstrained - V_concepts` is large. But the value network "reads who is ahead, not yet who will get ahead" (same passage), explains 30% of 180 s net value in v2 and nothing on the net-180 head in v3's late game ([docs/CONTROL.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/CONTROL.md:290), read in source). Large residuals will therefore concentrate in late, lopsided and long-game states — precisely the states underrepresented because pros resign — and the invented "concepts" will tend to name scoreboard correlates (army supply kept, games not ended) rather than missing strategic relations. Ellis et al., *DreamCoder*, PLDI 2021, https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381 (searched, not opened) and Grand et al., *LILO*, ICLR 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19791 (searched, not opened) both depend on an exact verifier to reject spurious library entries; the plan honestly notes BW lacks one (§5.1) but then makes paired-branch verification optional (§5.3 "candidate"). Make it mandatory and budgeted (30-50 branches per candidate concept, pre-written MDE) or Phase 3 will fill the library with residual artefacts. Also note Liang et al., *VisualPredicator*, ICLR 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23156 — opened via search record in this run — invents predicates *online during planning* in tabletop robotics; it supports the direction, not the offline residual-clustering mechanism, which remains Gary-specific and unproven.

### 14. Deleting/retiring working layers (§4.1) discards measured wins and contradicts the project's own adoption rule
Document section: §4.1 (`calm.py` DELETE, `army.py` REBUILD, `answers.py` RETIRE). Source: [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2273) — read in source.
The calm layer was folded into gt1dev (gt0.46dev) "for the standing measures it fixes" after CL282a moved first rest to the pros' own 8 s and standing stretches toward the pros; the commit-cluster fix (gt0.45dev, every build) is likewise adopted. AGENTS.md's rule (in the standing instructions) is that an adopted option is folded in and a stop-gap goes only when its measure says so with a learned layer holding the job. §4.1 retires these on architectural grounds before any squad-option provider has matched their measures. The same applies to `answers.py`: with `seenall` live since gt0.12 its own measure (option off against lurker/muta plans) is now runnable and has not been run. Require, per retired component, the AGENTS.md measure with the replacement on, in the Phase 2 gate — otherwise the refocus repeats the dev-build error of removing stop-gaps before the replacement holds their fidelity measure, which §2.2 itself criticises.

### 15. The TvZ-only pause conflicts with the architecture's first goal and with how the last trunk-adjacent bugs were actually found
Document section: §4.3 scope discipline and §6.2. Source: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:40) — read in source.
ARCHITECTURE §1 makes "every race, every matchup" constitutive, with TvZ "the first instance only" and race-specific code counted as debt; TODO's standing order is judgement "in every matchup, not in one matchup first" ([TODO.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/TODO.md:10), read in source) precisely because one matchup hid bugs others exposed (the stim model's Zerg-only fighter table hid 0% stim in PvT/TvT for its whole life until race-keying, [docs/PROGRESS.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/PROGRESS.md:2018) area and [gary/policy/stim.py](D:/docs/ai/bw/gary/policy/stim.py:108) area, read in source). A concept vocabulary and option set designed only on TvZ-Terran (lurker/muta beliefs, siege timing windows) is the highest-risk place to freeze contracts (`contracts.py`) that Zerg and Protoss must later fit. Weaker, safer discipline: build and gate the slice in TvZ, but require the `ConceptState`/`SquadOption` schemas to be *exercised* — even with trivial providers — by one Zerg and one Protoss dev game before freezing them, and keep `tests/test_matchups.py` plus per-matchup judge reads running. That preserves the falsification speed without baking Terran-shaped contracts.

### 16. Several "Verified References" (§7) are stretched past what the papers show, though the citations themselves check out
Document section: §7 and the Literature Grounding lists. Source: papers opened/searched in this run — inferred mapping, citations read from the document.
Verified accurate as cited: Alshiekh et al. 2018 (opened; preemptive/post-posed is theirs), Koh et al. 2020 (opened), Delfosse et al. 2024 SCoBots (opened; authors, venue and relational-concept claim correct), Sutton/Precup/Singh 1999 options (searched), Bacon/Harb/Precup Option-Critic 2017 (searched), Ross/Gordon/Bagnell DAgger 2011 quadratic compounding (searched; correctly attributed). Stretched: (a) Kautz, *The Third AI Summer*, AI Magazine 2022 (searched, not opened) — his taxonomy types (`Symbolic[Neuro]`, `Neuro|Symbolic`) classify integration patterns of a whole system; labelling Gary's tiers with two types at once adds no constraint and should not be presented as grounding for the sandwich's specific boundaries. (b) Silver et al., *Learning Neuro-Symbolic Skills for Bilevel Planning*, CoRL (proceedings 2023, arXiv 2022), https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10680 (searched, not opened) learns operators *and* samplers for search-then-sample planning with explicit effects; Gary's trunk does no search over operator sequences, so the support is analogical (skills under symbolic sequencing), not methodological — the plan's §5.1 table concedes the scale gap but not this mechanism gap. (c) Ontiveros et al., *Grounding Methods for Neural-Symbolic AI*, IJCAI 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08216 (searched, not opened) concerns grounding first-order logic programs (backward-chaining families); invoking its O(E^k) explosion for "200 units vs 1-4 squads" borrows the notation without the logic — squad abstraction is ordinary state aggregation and needs no NeSy grounding result. None of these invalidates the design; each should be downgraded from "grounding" to "analogy" so reviewers do not credit the plan with guarantees the papers do not give.

### 17. The plan underweights the strongest existing alternative for its own Phase 1 goal: AlphaStar-style z-conditioning of the current models
Document section: §3 Alternatives and §4.3 Phase 1. Source: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](D:/docs/ai/bw/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:82) — read in source.
The plan considers end-to-end RL and pure logic policies as the rejected alternatives, but not the middle path the architecture was originally built around: condition the existing `MacroNet`/`ArmyNet` on an explicit strategy statistic *z* (build-order tokens plus cumulative targets, AlphaStar's statistic; Vinyals et al., *Grandmaster level in StarCraft II using multi-agent reinforcement learning*, Nature 2019, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1724-z, searched not opened) and on a learned enemy-plan belief as an *input*, keeping decisions neural but making them steerable and recordable. Mathieu et al., *AlphaStar Unplugged: Large-Scale Offline Reinforcement Learning*, 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03526 (searched, not opened) found fine-tuned behaviour cloning plus single-step offline policy improvement beat plain BC by ~100 Elo on full-game SC2 offline data — evidence that Gary's BC stack has headroom *without* an interpretable scorer, and a baseline the concept-bottleneck scorer must beat on strength before its faithfulness premium is worth paying. Phase 1 should therefore run three arms on the same records: (i) counted-table trunk (plan Provider A), (ii) z-conditioned neural macro/army with belief input (no bottleneck), (iii) strict concept scorer — judged on style adherence, the five Q&As' decision-level faithfulness test (Finding 11), and 48 paired games. If (ii) matches (i)/(iii) on adherence and its decisions can be explained by citing *z* and belief records, the heavy Tier 2 scorer may be unnecessary for G3 and needed only for G5's finer questions. The plan's H4 only discovers this in Phase 3, after the trunk is built.

### 18. Smaller factual corrections that should not survive into v1.2
