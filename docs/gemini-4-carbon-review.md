### What the Literature Shows (All Searched and Opened in This Run)
- **Reconnaissance effort & negative evidence:** *Inferring Strategies from Limited Reconnaissance in Real-time Strategy Games*, Jesse Hostetler, Ethan Dereszynski, Tom Dietterich, & Alan Fern, 2012 (`https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.4880`, opened in this run) models hidden StarCraft tech/unit counts with a DBN + Beta-Binomial observation model over continuous **scouting effort** $E_t \in [0, 1]$ (*proportion of enemy main + natural area observed*), showing human scouting peaks sharply in the window where $P(\text{tech exists}) \times \text{expected info gain}$ is maximal (Fig. 2, §3.2, §5), and proving absence inference fails unless effort measures *area fraction covered* rather than point visits. Passive recognizers—*A Bayesian Model for Plan Recognition in RTS Games applied to StarCraft*, Gabriel Synnaeve & Pierre Bessière, 2011 (`https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.3735`, opened in this run) and *Forward Modeling for Partial Observation Strategy Games — A StarCraft Defogger*, Gabriel Synnaeve et al., 2018 (`https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.00054`, opened in this run)—never choose *when* to sense.
- **Value of Information vs. pure entropy or naive RL:** *Information Value Theory*, Ronald A. Howard, 1966 (`https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4082064`, abstract/record opened in this run) and *A POMDP Extension with Belief-dependent Rewards*, Mauricio Araya-López, Olivier Buffet, Vincent Thomas, & François Charpillet, 2010 (`https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2010/file/68053af2923e00204c3ca7c6a3150cf7-Paper.pdf`, opened in this run) establish that active sensing is optimal when expected decision-value gain (or convex belief uncertainty over *decision-relevant* states) exceeds sensing cost (50 Comsat energy needed later for burrowed Lurkers, or scout loss). Crucially, *A Robust and Opponent-Aware League Training Method for StarCraft II* (`ROA-Star`), Ruozi Huang et al., 2023 (`https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/94796017d01c5a171bdac520c199d9ed-Paper-Conference.pdf`, opened in this run, Footnote 5) documents that **AlphaStar's pure replay imitation + league RL failed to learn effective active scouting**, requiring explicit cross-entropy scouting rewards on 64 V100s + 4,600 CPUs—confirming scouting cannot be left implicit while RL is parked. Spatial staleness/coverage heuristics (*A Scouting Strategy for Real-Time Strategy Games*, Chen Si, Yusuf Pisan, & Chek Tien Tan, 2014, `https://opus.lib.uts.edu.au/bitstream/10453/41998/1/A%20Scouting%20Strategy%20for%20Real-time%20Strategy%20Games.pdf`, opened in this run; *A Particle Model for State Estimation in Real-Time Strategy Games*, Ben G. Weber, Michael Mateas, & Arnav Jhala, 2011, `https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/12424/12424-52-15952-1-2-20201228.pdf`, opened in this run) tell *where* staleness is highest once a scout/scan is triggered.

Track 4–6 base-rim sectors (or buildable-footprint coverage $E_t$ per Hostetler et al., 2012) instead of center distance.
2. **`train/plan_belief.py` `--sightings` era-leak bug (`lines 10, 286–303`) & `ingest/sighting_dataset.py` mirror crash (`lines 44, 85`):** `sighting_dataset.py` defaults to `--era modern`, while `plan_belief.py` fits `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` on **both eras (`16,480` games) with `"no era input"`** (`line 10`). With `--sightings`, all `14,049` old-era games get `nan_sight` (`NaN` × 7) while `2,431` modern games get finite values (`lines 291, 301`)—letting trees split on `is_nan` to recover the forbidden era indicator (Lurker share ~20% old vs. 5.6% modern)! Also, `sighting_dataset.py:85` (`[r for r in args.matchup.split("v") if r != args.race][0]`) crashes with `IndexError` on mirror matchups, `plan_belief.py:171–172` re-simulates each `--gary` replay *twice*, and `plan_belief.py:349–361` lacks both §3.1's ECE/per-class calibration and its Bayesian second provider.
3. **Macro Arm A breaks `--gg` (`eval/match.py:113–117`), `gary/policy/value.py:64–72`, and leaks future builds:** Appending card milestones + `PlanBelief` to `MacroNet`'s `FeatureSpec` causes `Resign.__init__` (`eval/match.py:114`) to reject the critic and print `"plays on"`—**silently running Arm A with `--gg 0` while Arm B resigns with `--gg`**, violating Gate 4 (`docs/REFOCUS.md:344`)—and breaks `no_style()`'s hardcoded index `7 + 2*len(own) + 2*len(seen) + len(decisions)` (`value.py:68`). Worse, training Arm A on realized future milestones from the *same* pro replay leaks the next click directly (*Causal Confusion in Imitation Learning*, Pim de Haan, Dinesh Jayaraman, & Sergey Levine, 2019, `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.11979`, opened in this run); *Grandmaster level in StarCraft II using multi-agent reinforcement learning* (`AlphaStar`), Oriol Vinyals et al., 2019 (`https://gwern.net/doc/reinforcement-learning/model-free/alphastar/2019-vinyals.pdf`, opened in this run) had to randomly zero `z` during supervised training (`0.5–0.8` keep probability), and conditioning on `PlanBelief` requires running `sighting_dataset.py` across all ~17k training games first.
4. **Macro Arm B (`docs/REFOCUS.md:180`) vs. `macro.py` queues & `DICE_FLOOR`:** `gt1dev` already reserves minerals/gas in `g.committed()` via `parkmany` (3 parked builds), `queued_builds`, and `gamequeue` (`gary/agent/macro.py:245, 318, 434–455, 525–545`). Subtracting `reserved_for_milestones` on top of `g.committed()` starves unit production; conversely, masking off-card tech (*Safe Reinforcement Learning via Shielding*, Mohammed Alshiekh et al., 2018, `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.08611`, opened in this run) will never trigger a directive item whose `MacroNet` probability is `< DICE_FLOOR` (`0.01`, `macro.py:375`) unless directives also enqueue goals or floor-boost permitted tech.
5. **§4.2 `contracts.py` signature gaps (`docs/REFOCUS.md:239–302`):** `StrategyTrunkProvider.step(belief, current, rng)` (`lines 300–301`) lacks `obs`/`mine`/`me`/`g.researched`, so it cannot evaluate milestone completion or `Directive.exit_condition`; `BranchScorerProvider.score(...)` (`lines 296–297`) lacks `map_id` and own-build context required by §3.2's hierarchical `min_n ≥ 25` backoff; `SightingState` (`lines 240–244`) collides in name/type with `gary/policy/sightings.py:26` and defines `unscouted` as `"never checked"` rather than stale; and `TacticalIntent` (`lines 279–290`) lacks `parent_id`/`trigger_record_id` for §3.5's join check.
