3. **G1 (Coherent Human-Like Play, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:53`) hits a fundamental latent-variable barrier under stateless/short-window marginal BC (read in source; mathematical cause inferred).**
   Human replay trajectories follow a mixture over persistent unobserved intentions, $p(a_t \mid s_t) = \sum_z p(a_t \mid s_t, z)\,p(z \mid h_t)$.    - Cross-layer conflict (`TODO.md:99–102, 122–123`): `ArmyNet` moves unsieged `ModeModel`'s tanks 29 times a game in PvT with no shared squad commitment, requiring pairwise patches (`staysieged`).

4. **Workstation Credit Assignment & Multi-Matchup Drift (`docs/PROGRESS.md:25–27, 38–39, 1796–1801, 2976`, `TODO.md:7–46, 143, 170`, read in source).**

### Finding 1: Solution 3's Pro-Replay Win-Rate Tables & Propensity Weighting Violate Overlap and Unconfoundedness, Recommending Survivor-Bias Mirages

- **Document section:** §1 Table (line 51), §3 Solution 3 (lines 213–227), §4.3 Phase 1.2 (line 379), §5.2 Q4 (line 425), §7 #21 (line 503).
- **Source:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:769–795`, `gary/agent/answers.py:1–120`, `gary/agent/selector.py:1–90`, `eval/pairs.py:152–195` (read in source; statistical failure mode inferred from source + domain structure).
- **Problem:** `docs/REFOCUS.md` conflates two distinct quantities under Provider A/C: *conditional pro behavior frequency* $P_{\text{pro}}(\text{response} \mid \text{sighting})$ (line 214: `*"after seeing Spire, pros build ≥1 turret/base within 60 s in p% (n)"*`) and *observational outcome win-rate* $\text{WR}(\text{branch} \mid \text{context})$ (line 51, line 223, line 425), invoking Counterfactual Risk Minimization (`Swaminathan & Joachims, 2015`) to deconfound replay outcomes. Both core assumptions of CRM fail in Brood War replays:
  1. **Positivity / overlap violation ($\pi_0(a \mid c) \approx 0$):** When pros scout a Spire on time, almost none choose `0 turrets` *unless* they are already winning inside the Zerg main. Observational $\text{WR}(\text{no\_turrets} \mid \text{spire\_seen})$ is therefore spuriously high, and inverse-propensity weights $1/\hat{\pi}_0(a \mid c)$ explode or remain biased under clipping.
  2. **Unobserved confounding:** Pro decisions respond to fine positional tempo (army spread, choke control, larva timing) absent from a 30–60 concept vector; `Swaminathan & Joachims (2015)` assumes logged known propensities and zero unobserved confounders.
- **Suggested improvement:**
  1. Strictly separate **imitation priors** from **outcome scoring**: use counted conditional pro frequencies $\hat{\pi}_{\text{pro}}(a \mid c)$ with an explicit **behavioral support constraint** ($\hat{\pi}_{\text{pro}}(a \mid c) \ge \tau$, e.g. $\ge 5\%$ and $n \ge 20$) to define the *feasible candidate branch set* and reactive defense norms (replacing `answers.py`).
  2. For offline outcome ranking within that supported set, replace raw IPS/CRM with **Doubly Robust estimation** (`Dudík, Langford, & Li, 2011`) paired with **Confounding-Robust Policy Improvement** (`Kallus & Zhou, 2018`), which optimizes worst-case regret against the pro behavioral baseline over a propensity-sensitivity uncertainty set $\Gamma$ so unobserved confounders cannot make low-support branches beat standard play.
  3. Promote §5.3 item 1 (paired simulator interventions in `eval/pairs.py:152–195` on 30–50 matched states) from "candidate future direction" into the **mandatory causal gate** whenever an outcome scorer wants to override the pro modal response.
- **Literature relied on:**
  - *Batch Learning from Logged Bandit Feedback through Counterfactual Risk Minimization*, Adith Swaminathan, Thorsten Joachims, 2015, `https://jmlr.org/papers/v16/swaminathan15a.html` (Plan §7 #21; opened in this run).
  - *Doubly Robust Policy Evaluation and Learning*, Miroslav Dudík, John Langford, Lihong Li, 2011, `https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.4601` (opened in this run).
  - *Confounding-Robust Policy Improvement*, Nathan Kallus, Angela Zhou, 2018, `https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08593` (opened in this run).

### Finding 5: Solution 2 & Open Question Q2 Miss Established Architectural Remedies for Soft-Probability Leakage and Correlated Opponent Beliefs

- **Document section:** §3 Solution 2 & 3 (lines 166–188, 214–216), §4.2 (lines 351–355), §5.2 Q2 (line 423), §7 #8, #10, #13, #15 (lines 490, 492, 495, 497).
- **Source:** `gary/policy/belief.py:131–140`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:756–766` (read in source).
- **Problem:** `docs/REFOCUS.md:423` poses Q2 as an open vulnerability (*"When Perception outputs continuous probabilities (`P(2h_muta) = 0.62`), can downstream learned branch scorers (`ebm_ga2m`, `concept_mlp`) remain genuinely faithful under intervention, or do they exploit subtle calibration correlations as hidden leakage?"*) while simultaneously proposing `concept_mlp` (Provider C, line 216) and independent multi-label tech heads (`early_pool`, `fast_3rd_hatch`, `spire_muta`, `lurker_tech`, line 168).
  1. As `Mahinpei et al. (2021)` and `Havasi, Parbhoo, & Doshi-Velez (2022)` prove, soft concept leakage arises *because* a flexible label predictor (`concept_mlp` or unconstrained boosted trees) is trained **jointly or sequentially on continuous predicted probabilities $\hat{p}(c)$**, allowing it to decode out-of-concept features hidden in sub-percent variations (`0.014` vs. `0.029`).
  2. Predicting independent marginal probabilities over mutually constrained Zerg plans (`2-hatch muta` vs. `3-hatch lurker`) breaks joint coherence and corrupts test-time interventions (`Havasi et al., 2022`): intervening `P(spire_muta) = 0` leaves `P(lurker_tech)` unchanged unless Tier 1 models their joint correlation.
- **Suggested improvement:**
  1. Eliminate soft-probability leakage **by construction**: use **Independent CBM training** (`Koh et al., 2020`), fitting Tier 2 scorers (`counted_table`, `ebm_ga2m`) strictly on **ground-truth binary/quantized concepts from resim** (never on Tier 1's predicted $\hat{p}(c)$), enforce **monotonicity constraints** per concept in `ebm_ga2m` (`Nori et al., 2019`), and evaluate expected branch scores at runtime by integrating/sampling over calibrated concept posteriors (`Havasi et al., 2022`). Drop `concept_mlp` (`Provider C`, line 216), which adds expressive leakage capacity without interpretability.
  2. Separate **fog-induced ambiguity / epistemic uncertainty** from point probabilities via **Probabilistic Concept Bottleneck Models (`ProbCBM`, `Kim et al., 2023`)**, and predict a **joint categorical distribution over the opponent's taxonomy plan $P(z_{\text{enemy}} \mid \text{seen})$** (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:758–765`) from which correlated tech and timing probabilities derive coherently under Bayes' rule.
- **Literature relied on:**
  - *Concept Bottleneck Models*, Pang Wei Koh, Thao Nguyen, Yew Siang Tang, Stephen Mussmann, Emma Pierson, Been Kim, Percy Liang, 2020, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.04612` (Plan §7 #10; opened in this run).
  - *Promises and Pitfalls of Black-Box Concept Learning Models*, Anita Mahinpei, Justin Clark, Isaac Lage, Finale Doshi-Velez, Weiwei Pan, 2021, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.13314` (Plan §7 #13; opened in this run).
  - *Addressing Leakage in Concept Bottleneck Models*, Marton Havasi, Sonali Parbhoo, Finale Doshi-Velez, 2022, `https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/944ecf65a46feb578a43abfd5cddd960-Paper-Conference.pdf` (Plan §7 #8; opened in this run).
  - *InterpretML: A Unified Framework for Machine Learning Interpretability*, Harsha Nori, Samuel Jenkins, Paul Koch, Rich Caruana, 2019, `https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09223` (Plan §7 #15; opened in this run).

### Finding 7: Tier 3 Leaf Learning Relies on Myopic Sub-Episode Rewards and Non-Existent `DAgger` Oracles Instead of Action-Chunked Generative BC and Critic-Bootstrapped Offline RL (`IQL`)

- **Document section:** §3 Solution 4 (lines 240–257), §4.3 Phase 2.3 (lines 389–390), §6.1 H3 (line 453), §7 #17 (line 499).
- **Source:** `TODO.md:29–32, 170` (`#53`), `gary/agent/manifests.py:756–761, 995–999`, `docs/PROGRESS.md:35, 2201–2236` (read in source).
- **Problem:**
  1. **No `DAgger` oracle exists (lines 251, 453):** H3's fallback (`docs/REFOCUS.md:453`) proposes *"`DAgger`-style filtered imitation"*, citing `Ross, Gordon, & Bagnell (2011)`. `DAgger` requires an **interactive expert $\pi^*(s)$** querying labels on states visited by the learner's own rollouts. Gary has no human labeling millions of 0.5-second states and no legal human-constrained bot oracle (`Pluto` is binary-only and superhuman-bandwidth, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:73–75`).
  2. **Myopic 15–45 s rewards already failed full-game transfer (`TODO.md:170`, #53):** Solution 4 (line 245) rewards 15–45 s sub-episodes with purely local metrics (*"survival + distance gained"*, *"local fight trade"*). In Gary's own history (`gary/agent/manifests.py:756–761`, `TODO.md:170`), fight RL runs `rl2` and `rl3` beat `rl1` on 20-second local fight trade (`+255`, `+268` vs. `+195`) yet **lost in full games** because local combat trade ignores positional value, ramp/base defense, and unit-composition survival at $t + \tau$.
  3. **0.5 s memoryless leaf classification still jitters inside an option:** Even conditioned on `TIMING_PUSH` or `HARASS`, predicting single-step `0.5 s` marginal actions averages across distinct human micro sequences (step-and-shoot rhythms, flank arcs, focus-fire sequences).
- **Suggested improvement:**
  1. Upgrade Tier 3 (`RoleNet` and `MacroNet` unit/build sequences) from 1-step Markov draws to **Action-Chunked / Sequence-Generative Behavioral Cloning** (`ACT`, `Zhao et al., 2023`; `Behavior Transformers`, `Shafiullah et al., 2022`), predicting short $2\text{--}4\text{ s}$ multi-step action chunks conditioned on the active `SquadOption` (with temporal ensembling and immediate preemption on option change)—eliminating retreat cancellation and multimodal averaging purely from offline replays without RL or an interactive expert.
  2. Replace raw local sub-episode rewards in Phase 2.3 with **potential-based terminal critic bootstrapping** $R_{t:t+\tau} = \sum_{k=0}^{\tau-1} \gamma^k r_{t+k} + \gamma^\tau V_{\text{game}}(s_{t+\tau}) - V_{\text{game}}(s_t)$ using `ValueNet`, and run **Implicit Q-Learning (`IQL`, `Kostrikov, Nair, & Levine, 2021`)** offline on option-segmented replay + league trajectories before spending online rollout compute.
- **Literature relied on:**
  - *A Reduction of Imitation Learning and Structured Prediction to No-Regret Online Learning*, Stéphane Ross, Geoffrey J. Gordon, J. Andrew Bagnell, 2011, `https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686` (Plan §7 #17; opened in this run).
  - *Learning Fine-Grained Bimanual Manipulation with Low-Cost Hardware* (`ACT`), Tony Z. Zhao, Vikash Kumar, Sergey Levine, Chelsea Finn, 2023, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13705` (opened in this run).
  - *Behavior Transformers: Cloning $k$ modes with one stone* (`BeT`), Nur Muhammad Mahi Shafiullah, Zichen Jeff Cui, Ariuntuya Altanzaya, Lerrel Pinto, 2022, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11251` (opened in this run).
  - *Offline Reinforcement Learning with Implicit Q-Learning* (`IQL`), Ilya Kostrikov, Ashvin Nair, Sergey Levine, 2021, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06169` (opened in this run).

### Finding 8: Solution 5 Overlooks Fast Post-Hoc Concept Subspace Screening (`PCBM`) and Guide Priors Before Expensive Clustering, and Risks Self-Play Residual Pollution

- **Document section:** §3 Solution 5 (lines 266–280), §4.3 Phase 3 (lines 394–396).
- **Source:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:767–826`, `gary/policy/value.py:1–120`, `taxonomy/discover.py:1–150` (read in source).
- **Problem:** Solution 5 (line 268) jumps directly from `V_unconstrained - V_concepts` residuals to latent clustering plus LLM/human inspection of example replays, and Phase 3.1 (line 394) mixes held-out pro replays with *"self-play paired branches"* when measuring and mining residuals.
  1. Computing residuals on Gary-vs-Gary self-play will surface missing concepts that exploit `gz1`/`gt_ns` blind spots rather than human Brood War structure (noted as a risk on line 279, but unguarded in Phase 3.1–3.2).
  2. It skips two much faster candidate-concept generators: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §7.6 step 3a & §7.7 (`lines 781, 791–826`, screening programmatic candidate features and structured guide claims first) and **Post-hoc Concept Bottleneck Models (`PCBM`, `Yuksekgonul, Wang, & Zou, 2022`)**, which project a frozen `ValueNet` embedding onto Concept Activation Vectors learned from tiny annotated slices (`50–100` states) and fit a sparse residual model (`PCBM-h`) in seconds on CPU without retraining the backbone.
- **Suggested improvement:** Insert a fast **PCBM + Guide-Claim Subspace Screen** ahead of latent clustering in Phase 3.2: extract candidate concepts from Brood War guides (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:798–814`) and geometric predicates, test each candidate's `PCBM` projection against held-out **modern pro replay** residuals (`V_unconstrained - V_concepts`) with Holm-Bonferroni correction, and use `eval/pairs.py` self-play branches strictly as the *downstream causal validation step* after a concept passes pro-replay residual reduction.
- **Literature relied on:**
  - *Post-hoc Concept Bottleneck Models*, Mert Yuksekgonul, Maggie Wang, James Zou, 2022, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15480` (opened in this run).
