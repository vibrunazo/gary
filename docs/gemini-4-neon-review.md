**Comparing candidate designs, literature, and `AGENTS.md` compliance:**
- *Literature:* Synnaeve & Bessière (2011) and Weber & Mateas (2009) address passive ("keyhole") build-tree recognition from replays, while Hostetler et al. (2012) formalize strategy inference under limited reconnaissance. Turning scouting into an active decision draws on Value of Information (Howard, 1966; Krause & Guestrin, 2009—gather observations that change the downstream decision, not blind entropy reduction), belief-dependent $\rho$-POMDPs (Araya et al., 2010), and Active Goal Recognition over landmarks (Shvo & McIlraith, 2020). Brood War scout micro/pathing precedents (Nguyen et al., 2013; Si et al., 2014; Ontañón et al., 2013) keep scouting modular.
- *Candidate A — Pro timings sampled (open-loop table per style/map):* Easy to build and avoids a fixed clock tick, but wastes scans when the scout already saw Spire and misses re-scans when a first scan missed. Under `AGENTS.md`: **a stop-gap** (like `gp0.15dev`'s placement table before `PlacementModel`, `manifests.py:1556`).
- *Candidate B — Trigger on unscouted / belief uncertainty (VoI / Active Goal Recognition):* If a decision-critical region (`enemy_main`, `natural`, `third`) is `unscouted`/stale past a pro-calibrated window *and* `P(enemy_z)` still splits across directives that require different responses (e.g., Muta vs. Lurker), spend 50 Comsat energy (or dispatch a free scout/Vulture). Closed-loop and maximally explainable for G5 (*"scanned main at 5:35 because tech was unscouted and `P(lurker)` vs. `P(muta)` was unresolved"*). Under `AGENTS.md`: **still a stop-gap** (hand-coded staleness/entropy thresholds; must be registered with an exit measure and learned replacement).
- *Candidate C — Learned scouting model (imitation of pros' scans + air/scout clusters):* **NOT a stop-gap under `AGENTS.md`**, and matches Gary's proven pipeline: `train/cast.py` (`PROGRESS.md:3452-3472`) already learns when/where pros cast spells from replays, `gp0.23dev` (`aircluster`, `manifests.py:1573-1577`) already moved Corsair scouting via `ArmyNet`, and `AGENTS.md:269` + `TODO.md:95` explicitly designate **Comsat scans as a learned decision**. Feeding `SightingState` (time since main/natural/third looked at) + `PlanBelief` + Comsat energy + style into a learned scan event model (same `train/modes.py`/`cast.py` family) learns *both* pro timings and closed-loop VoI, and generalizes across races (Terran scans, Protoss Observers/Corsairs, Zerg Overlords).
- **Recommendation:** (1) Fix `army.py:1030` (`enemy_main` set unconditionally—a plain bug fix under `AGENTS.md`, no round required). ## 2. Card and Belief Beyond 97–100% Style Adherence and `answers.py`

**What 97–100% style adherence (`eval/style_adherence.py`) actually counts—and misses:**
Read `eval/style_adherence.py:2-4, 26, 37-39, 114-124` and `taxonomy/discover.py:51-60, 82-94`. For TvZ Terran, `style_adherence.py` encodes each game as a 23-dim vector of the **first timestamp within 8:00** of key items (`rax1..3, gas1, cc2, factory, starport, ...`, missing = 10:00) and runs a **15-NN classifier** over 4 coarse all-era HDBSCAN tech clusters (`1: standard bio`, `2: Factory and vultures`, `3: Factory and Starport`, `4: 2-Rax marines`). Thus 97–100% proves only that told style 1, `gt1dev` opens bio rather than Factory-first, and told style 2, it builds a Factory and Shop before 8:00. It **does not** measure:
- **Sub-style or replay fidelity:** Style 1 collapses every modern bio opening (64% of pro TvZ) into one bucket; you cannot steer Gary to follow a specific replay (`--from-replay`) or distinguish 1-Rax FE from 2-Rax Academy.
- **Per-game timing variance:** Reports only median timings, not per-run tolerance (`±10–15 s` in 18/20 strict drills, `ARCHITECTURE.md:238, 456`).
- **Composition after first item or play after 8:00:** In live game `p0_t` (`PROGRESS.md:2118-2123`), style 2 built 16 Vultures, 10 Goliaths, and 2 Tanks while banking 1,046 gas—yet still labels as style 2.
- **Zerg and Protoss drift:** `gz1dev` hits only **56%** style adherence (`manifests.py:1438`); `gp1dev` hits 50% PvT and 19% PvP citadel-first (`TODO.md:210`).

**What `answers.py` (`gary/agent/answers.py:14-182`) covers—and misses:**
- *Covers:* Deterministic, binary triggers on seeing Spire/Muta (`"mutas"` → Ebay, Academy, 2 mineral-line turrets/base, vetoes non-mineral turrets) or Lurker/Den+Lair (`"lurkers"` → 10-step Factory/Shop/Siege/2 Tanks/Starport/Tower/Facility/2 Vessels/Irradiate/4 Tanks, caps turrets at 1/base).
- *Misses:* (1) **Zero opening/economy inference (0:00–6:00):** ignores `pool first` vs. `hatchery first`, `2h` vs. `3h`, and `zergling pressure`—leaving `gt1dev` (`noalarm`, `manifests.py:1399`) completely blind to early pools and ling floods; (2) **no probabilistic anticipation or confirmed-absence inference** (never reacts before seeing Spire/Den/Lurker directly); (3) **permanent lock-in bug (`answers.py:77-81`):** `if SPIRE/MUTA ... elif LURKER/DEN+LAIR` means once a Zerg opens Mutas and transitions to Lurkers, `answers.py` stays stuck on `"mutas"` forever and never adds Siege/Vessels; (4) **Terran TvZ-only and style-blind** (forces bio-oriented lists even in mech/starport styles) with no record of evidence or alternatives.

**Smallest card and belief that add measurable value—and the measure that shows it:**
- **Minimal Belief:** Joint `P(enemy_z | sightings, time, map)` over `taxonomy/plans.py`'s 3 parts (`opening`, `hatcheries`, `tech`) paired with explicit `SightingState` (`seen`, `confirmed_absent`, `unscouted` per region & tech). Note `runs/plan_belief/TvZ_T_20261123-131911/report.json:40-220`: on pros, the belief's biggest wins over `answers.py` are `opening` (`-0.083` Brier at 4:00) and `hatcheries` (`-0.083/-0.128/-0.132` at 4/6/8 min)—both invisible to `answers.py`—plus beating `answers.py` on `tech` at 8:00 (`0.036` vs. `0.052`).
- **Minimal Card + Directives:** (1) An ordered **6–10 milestone opening card** (key structures/expansions/techs + target times from a replay or style template; leave continuous workers/units/depots to `MacroNet`); (2) **three named reactive directives** (`DEFEND_EARLY_POOL`, `PREP_VS_MUTA`, `PREP_VS_LURKER`—the last two allowed to co-exist/transition, replacing `answers.py`'s lock-in!), scored by `P_pro(directive | belief, context)` with `n` and explicit sighting/record IDs.
- **Measures that prove added value:** (1) **Per-milestone timing error (MAE and p90 lag vs. target replay card)** plus `≥18/20` unharassed strict drills within written tolerance; (2) **vs. the 4-archetype Zerg suite (plus a Muta→Lurker transition):** time from first diagnostic sighting to defensive/tech response started (ramp bunker vs. 9-pool, mineral-line turret before Mutas arrive vs. 2h Muta, Siege+Vessel vs. 3h Lurker and Muta→Lurker), and `PlanBelief` Brier beating *both* the prior and `answers.py`; (3) **blind Q&A prediction accuracy and coaching usefulness** over today's `gt1dev` records.

## Additional Findings (Most Important First)

1. **§3.2's `min_n ≥ 25` support floor fails on 3 of the 4 scripted Zerg archetypes (`REFOCUS.md` §3.1 line 151, §3.2 line 163; `runs/plan_belief/TvZ_T_20261123-131911/report.json:2-3, 111-115`; read in source + inferred):**
   The actual TvZ belief training set has **2,431** modern games (`2,356` running at 4:00; `REFOCUS.md:151` cites 2,683 and `PROGRESS.md:2197` cites 3,059—three conflicting totals), of which **93.8%** are spire-first, **5.8% (~137)** lurker-first, **0.2% (5 games)** hydralisks, and **0.2% (5 games)** zergling pressure. Both `zergling pressure` and `hydralisks` have `n = 5 < 25` globally, and `lurker first` falls below 25 once conditioned on map or Terran style. Without an explicit backoff to all-era counts (weighted, `docs/DATA_ERAS.md`) or marginal plan parts (`opening` / `hatcheries` / `tech`), `P_pro` collapses to the global spire-first majority on every non-spire archetype.
2. **Hidden inheritance error repeated five times (`REFOCUS.md` §2.1 lines 72–73, §3.3 lines 173 & 177, §4.1 line 229, §6 line 383; `gary/agent/manifests.py:849, 855, 1224, 1398-1399`; read in source):**
   `REFOCUS.md` lists `natural_bunker` as still active in `gt1dev` (and line 73 includes `posture.build`). In source, `gt1` sets `natural_bunker=False` via `+nonatbunker` (`manifests.py:849, 1224`) and `gt1dev` inherits it (`line 1398`), while `+noalarm` disables `posture.build` (`lines 855, 1399`). Conversely, line 72 omits `odds_mass`, which *is* active in `gt1dev` (`manifests.py:1224`; `macro.py:396`).
3. **Gate 4's rewritten margin uses a mismatched `--gg 0` variance and tolerates large regressions (`REFOCUS.md` §4.3 line 344; `PROGRESS.md:2153-2166, 2430`; read in source + inferred):**
   Importing `RD285a`/`RP285a`'s per-pair SD (`0.466`, yielding MDE `0.17` at 48 games) from a `--gg 0` `gt1dev`-vs-`gt1` pairing against `gz1` to gate a `--gg 0.1` `gt0.50dev`-vs-arm pairing against `gz1dev` mixes different outcome distributions (`CB280a` had 16/16 games decided by gg vs. 60/96 undecided in `RD285a`). Worse, an MDE of `0.17` at 48 games cannot distinguish a `-0.14` regression from zero; specify the maximum tolerable loss (e.g., `-0.10`) and run one 96-paired-game check at Phase 1 exit instead of pretending 48 games guard strength.
4. **Boosted-tree belief without scouting dropout will break under distribution shift even after scouting improves (`REFOCUS.md` §3.1 lines 147–151; `train/plan_belief.py:56, 323`; read in source + inferred):**
   `train/plan_belief.py` only built boosted trees, not the Bayesian provider (Synnaeve & Bessière, 2011; Hostetler et al., 2012). Under a structured Bayesian observation model, `unscouted` regions multiply the likelihood by 1 (clean no-update regardless of how often the agent scouts), whereas boosted trees trained only on pro scouting rates still overfit pro-specific absence correlations. Either prioritize the Bayesian provider first or train the tree model with **random scouting-visibility dropout** (masking base looks to `unscouted` across 5%–100% rates) so calibration holds across any scouting level.
5. **Gate 3's 100% 1 ms abductive flip remains a smoke check, not an acceptance gate (`REFOCUS.md` §4.3 line 342; `REFOCUSReviews.md:225`; read in source):**
   Already flagged in `REFOCUSReviews.md` 1.11 and not revised in `82947cd`. On a deterministic trunk, reverting the triggering evidence trivially flips 100% of switches. Demote to a unit test (`tests/test_trunk.py`); weight Gate 3 on execution-join integrity (≥95%) and blind human evaluation.
6. **§3.3 soak metric `on_card`/`off_card` is undefined for continuous production (`REFOCUS.md` §3.3 line 176; `gary/agent/macro.py:364-513`; inferred from source):**
   ~85% of macro decisions are continuous production (SCVs, Marines, Depots) not listed on a milestone card. Define `on_card`/`off_card` strictly on **one-off milestones** (tech buildings, add-ons, expansions, research) and measure milestone lag plus unrequested one-off draws. Similarly, write down the exact strict-drill timing tolerance (`±10 s` or `±15 s`, which milestones) in §4.3 Gate 2 before running the 20 drills.
7. **Unverified item settled + stale baseline/Phase 0 references (`REFOCUS.md` §3.4 lines 187, 190–191, §4.3 lines 314, 339, §5.1 lines 354–358; `gary/policy/army.py:40-74, 135-156, 358-405`; read in source):**
   §3.4/§5.1 asks to *"verify whether `commit` already receives research state as an input."* **Settled in source: it does not.** `ArmySpec` and `encode_global()` (`gary/policy/army.py:40-74, 135-156`) include only `[frame, minerals, gas, supply, group_sup, own_army, enemy_army_mem, unit/building counts, commit, others, air]`—no research or upgrade state (so `commit` cannot see Stim Packs, +1, Siege Mode, or Spider Mines progress).
