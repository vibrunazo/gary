## Is a Major Refocus Necessary, or Will the Current Direction Get There?

**Verdict: A structural refocus is necessary—more bug fixing and per-layer imitation training under the current direction will not reach Gary's core goals (G1, G3, G4, G5)—but the refocus needed is a disciplined return to `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` rather than the over-scoped redesign proposed in `docs/REFOCUS.md`.**

Four repo-verified facts establish why the current direction (`TODO.md` L7–46, `AGENTS.md`) cannot converge to `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §1 (L51–58):

1. **G3 (Steerable), G4 (Adjustable skill), and G5 (Explainable) are structurally absent, not merely unfinished** `[Read in source]`. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §1 (L55–57) defines G3 as compiling a prompt or replay into a `StrategySpec` (*z*) followed reliably, G4 as a multi-skill `HumanProfile` (*h*), and G5 as a queryable causal trace of intent, beliefs, alternatives, and hand constraints. Polishing fight decoders across six matchups adds zero capability toward G3, G4, or G5.
2. **Stateless per-tick marginal behavior cloning hits a structural ceiling on G1 (human-like coherence)** `[Read in source + Inferred]`. Marginalizing over unobserved human intent turns multi-modal trajectories into incoherent jitter. `CalmNet` proved this empirically (`docs/PROGRESS.md` L2197–2274): it matched pros' short-order rate (1.43 vs. 1.58/min) and new-unit rest (8 s vs. 8 s), yet its direction head scored log-loss 2.669 vs. 2.729 bin-prior / 2.773 uniform, scattering units (`units alone` rose 0.29→0.41 vs. pros' 0.20; army spread 16.7→21.8 vs. 14.0). 3. **Short-horizon RL on workstation compute has already hit the SNR floor** `[Read in source]`. ## Findings (Most Important First)

### 1. Soft-Probability "Strict Concept Bottleneck" Does Not Prevent Leakage, Misapplies Havasi et al. (2022), and Ignores Better StarCraft & CBM Methods
- **Document:** §3 Sol 2 (L167–178, L183, L187), §4.2 (L351–366), §5.2 Q1–Q2 (L422–423), §7 Ref 8 (L490).
- **Source:** `gary/policy/belief.py` L1–140; `train/belief.py` L61–65; `docs/PROGRESS.md` L38.
- **Provenance:** Read in source (`belief.py`, `PROGRESS.md`); architectural critique inferred from `REFOCUS.md` and verified literature.
- **Problem:** §3 Sol 2 (L172) mandates a *"Strict Bottleneck Rule at Runtime: The Strategic Trunk's branch scorer receives **only** these named symbolic concepts and calibrated probabilities—no raw hidden-layer bypass vectors,"* while passing continuous soft probabilities `P(enemy_concepts | seen)` into learned scorers (`ebm_ga2m`, `concept_mlp`, L353). Yet **Havasi, Parbhoo, & Doshi-Velez (2022)** (cited at L177, L187, L490) showed that passing *soft probabilities* from an incomplete concept set into a learned downstream predictor is precisely what produces concept leakage and broken test-time interventions—and their actual remedy was **adding explicit side channels** to soak up non-concept residual signal so concepts stay clean, not banning side channels while keeping soft probabilities! Second, StarCraft opponent inference under fog-of-war suffers severe distribution shift when purely discriminative (`belief.py` error doubles from 5.9 on pros to 11.7 on league games, `docs/PROGRESS.md` L38).
- **Better literature & concrete fix:**
  1. Split Tier 1 into **(a) deterministic/Bayesian tech-tree & timing inference** for hard game-rule constraints (e.g., Lair cannot finish before Pool + build time)—grounded in Brood War plan-recognition literature (**Synnaeve & Bessière, 2011**; **Hostetler et al., 2012**), which stays calibrated under Gary-vs-Gary distribution shift—and **(b) uncertainty-aware neural perception** for soft tactical predicates (**ProbCBM**, Kim et al., 2023).
  2. If Tier 2 uses a neural scorer (`concept_mlp`), train with **simulated test-time interventions** (**CEM**, Espinosa Zarlenga et al., 2022; **IntCEM**, Espinosa Zarlenga et al., 2023) or pass **discretized/thresholded concept states** into `counted_table`/`ebm_ga2m` so soft calibration artifacts cannot smuggle raw state past the bottleneck.
  3. Note a source error at L167: `gary/policy/belief.py` does *not* predict *"only raw enemy army supply"*—`train/belief.py` (L61–65) trains expected counts for every army type, workers, and town halls, and `BeliefModel.predict` (`gary/policy/belief.py` L135–140) already computes per-type `counts` and quantiles `q50`/`q80` (it simply drops the last two indices—workers and town halls—from the returned dict at L139). Exposing them is a one-line win before building a new dataset.

### 2. Nine Hand-Authored `SquadOptions` + Heuristic Replay Relabeling Recreate Brittle Scripting and Miss Modern Action-Chunking / Offline-HRL Solutions
- **Document:** §3 Sol 3 (L203–209, L228), §3 Sol 4 (L240, L255–256), §4.1 (L334–335), §4.3 Phase 2 (L383–391).
- **Source:** `AGENTS.md` L163–172 (`gather`); `gary/agent/manifests.py` L701–706 (`join`/`sweep`/`press`); `docs/CONTROL.md` L201–205 (abandoned per-command micro labels); `docs/PROGRESS.md` L1200–1216 (`holdtarget`, `unitcommits`, pro 30 s redirect rate 0.79 vs. `gt1dev` 0.70), L2197–2274 (`CalmNet`), L2409–2411 (retreats).
- **Provenance:** Read in source (`AGENTS.md`, `manifests.py`, `CONTROL.md`, `PROGRESS.md`); alternative architecture inferred from literature.
- **Problem:** Phase 2 replaces `Commit` + `Calm` with 9 hand-authored persistent options (`STAGE`, `REGROUP`, `TIMING_PUSH`, `CONTAIN`, `HARASS`, `DEFEND`, `DISENGAGE`, `SCOUT`, `CLEAR_FINISH`, L204) held 10–60 s (L329), and trains option-conditioned `RoleNet` on heuristically option-labeled pro replays (L255–256). Two repo findings warn against this:
  1. **Gary's own rigid tactical wrappers failed:** `gather` moved late arrivals only 0.26→0.22 and lost wins (47 vs. 54, `AGENTS.md` L163–172); `join`/`sweep`/`press` worsened Zerg-ahead games (`manifests.py` L701–706); `holdtarget` and `unitcommits` (`-0.048`) helped nothing because **pros themselves redirect 0.79 of group orders within 30 s** (`gt1dev` 0.70, `docs/PROGRESS.md` L1210–1216). Hard 10–60 s option locks risk sluggish, bot-like armies.
  2. **The replay-labeling bottleneck is unsolved:** Heuristically distinguishing pro `CONTAIN` vs. `TIMING_PUSH` vs. `STAGE` vs. `HARASS` across millions of unit frames in `ingest/response_dataset.py` will inject severe label noise (the same reason Gary abandoned per-command micro intent labels in `docs/CONTROL.md` L201–205). Noisy labels cause `RoleNet(s | option)` to ignore the option token or collapse off-distribution.
- **Better literature & concrete fix:**
  - The root cause of `CalmNet`'s Brownian motion and 2 s retreat cancellations is **stateless 1-step marginal prediction over multimodal trajectories**, not missing 9 hand-coded option state machines.
  - Replace heuristic 9-way micro option labels with **directly observable spatial/temporal conditioning**:
    1. Keep symbolic Tier 2 squad intents **small and crisp** (5–6 event-grounded modes: `DEFEND(base)`, `ATTACK/PUSH(region)`, `HARASS(target)`, `STAGE(region)`, `DISENGAGE`, `CLEAR_FINISH`) for coaching explanations and 30–90 s strategic pacing.
    2. For Tier 3 continuous movement (`Calm` + `RoleNet`), condition on **observable future squad waypoints + action chunks** rather than noisy symbolic tags—using **Action Chunking (`ACT`, Zhao et al., 2023)** or **residual vector-quantized latent skills (`VQ-BeT`, Lee et al., 2024; `OPAL`, Ajay et al., 2021; `SPiRL`, Pertsch et al., 2020)** trained unsupervised on pro unit-group trajectories (zero heuristic relabeling, native multi-modality, coherent 2–6 s retreat/reposition arcs).
    3. For workstation-budget offline hierarchical RL over squad waypoints without multi-level non-stationarity, evaluate **HIQL (Park et al., 2023)**, which extracts high-level subgoals and goal-conditioned low-level policies offline from an action-free value function (directly leveraging Gary's `ValueNet`).

### 3. Counterfactual Risk Minimization (Swaminathan & Joachims, 2015) Cannot Deconfound Observational Pro Replay Win Rates
- **Document:** §3 Sol 3 (L214, L216, L223), §5.2 Q4 (L425), §7 Ref 21 (L503).
- **Source:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.8 (L508–516); `gary/decision_log.py` L94–115.
- **Provenance:** Read in source (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, `decision_log.py`); statistical critique inferred from verified literature.
- **Problem:** §3 Sol 3 (L223) claims **Swaminathan & Joachims (2015)** *"establish propensity-weighted offline policy evaluation and variance regularization for learning from **confounded observational logs**—directly addressing `ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.8's confounding warning on pro replay win rates."* That misstates the method's assumptions. CRM / IPS requires **(1) known logging propensities** $\pi_0(a|x)$, **(2) strict positivity/overlap**, and **(3) unconfoundedness (ignorability)**. Pro Brood War replays violate all three: propensities are unknown, rare counters lack overlap, and pros pick all-ins/transitions using *unobserved* confounders (player skill gap, series mind-games, micro confidence, subtle scouting reads). Estimated inverse propensities cannot remove unobserved confounding and can blow up variance.
- **Concrete fix:**
  1. Use pro replays strictly as a **descriptive behavioral prior** (what pros build/respond with and when: e.g., sighting-to-response frequencies, which are *not* outcome-confounded).
  2. Apply **Doubly Robust Off-Policy Evaluation (Jiang & Li, 2016)** *only* to Gary's own stochastic self-play logs where propensities $\pi_0$ are known and logged (`gary/decision_log.py` L94–115), paired with `ValueNet` as the regression baseline.
  3. Elevate `eval/pairs.py` targeted interventional branches (currently relegated to Phase 3 §5.3.1, L432–433) to be the **primary causal verifier** for Tier 2 strategy selection tables.

### 4. The `eval/pairs.py` "Counterfactual Replay Verifier" Conflates Policy Sensitivity with Game-Outcome Causality and Misses StarCraft-Tested XRL
- **Document:** §3 Sol 6 (L298), §4.1 (L325), §4.3 Phase 1 (L380), §6.1 H1 (L451).
- **Source:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §8 (L842–876); `gary/policy/attribution.py` L72–95; `eval/pairs.py` L1–45.
- **Provenance:** Read in source (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, `attribution.py`, `pairs.py`); design critique inferred from `REFOCUS.md` and verified literature.
- **Problem:** §3 Sol 6 (L298) proposes: *"Leverage Gary's deterministic branching engine (`eval/pairs.py`) as a **counterfactual verifier**: if an explanation cites concept/fact $F$ as decisive at frame $t$, replay frame $t$ with $F$ intervened/flipped and verify that the trunk's chosen branch actually changes."* This conflates two completely different checks:
  1. **Single-step policy sensitivity does not need `eval/pairs.py`.** `eval/pairs.py` (L1–45) snapshots and restores OpenBW for 20–90 s two-arm rollouts; checking whether flipping $F$ in `ConceptState` at frame $t$ flips `StrategyTrunkProvider.step(concepts, current)` is a 1 ms stateless call on logged state (just like `gary/policy/attribution.py` L72–95 and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` L876). Worse, when `BranchScorerProvider` is `ebm_ga2m` or `counted_table`, per-concept contributions are already algebraically exact—checking whether flipping the top additive term changes the argmax is largely tautological and does not test whether the explanation is strategically true.
  2. **Flipping belief $F$ inside a live OpenBW rollout tests hallucination sensitivity, not coaching validity.** Changing Gary's belief `P(muta)` without changing the opponent's actual Spire only tests whether Gary reacts to a false belief.
  3. **Learners need consequence explanations, not only feature triggers.** In a user study of **120 humans on StarCraft II**, **Madumal et al. (2020)** showed that contrastive causal explanations describing *expected consequences* (*"I added Tanks instead of Marines because without Tanks, Lurkers deny my natural"*) significantly outperform feature-trigger explanations on human task prediction, satisfaction, and trust (**Milani et al., 2024** similarly stresses human mental-model evaluation over algorithmic consistency alone).
- **Concrete fix:** Separate three distinct checks: (1) **decision-record faithfulness** via exact algebraic EBM/table attribution or 1 ms `ConceptState` perturbation (no OpenBW replay), (2) **consequence counterfactuals** via `eval/pairs.py` + `ValueNet` comparing *action* A vs. *action* B (*"holding instead of pushing drops 90 s win odds from 61% to 44%"*), and (3) a **blind human task-prediction check** in Phase 1 (can the player predict Gary's next adaptation from its explanation?).

- **Source:** `gary/agent/answers.py` L133–182; `gary/agent/manifests.py` L1700–1730; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.7 (L454–473).

### 8. Phase 3 Automated Predicate Discovery Is Premature, Timelines Are 3× Optimistic, and G4 (`HumanProfile`) Is Deferred Too Late

- **Problem:**
  1. **Phase 3 over-reaches SOTA:** As §5.1 (L413–415) candidly admits, **LILO (Grand et al., 2024)** relies on exact deterministic program verifiers and **VisualPredicator (Liang et al., 2025)** runs on single-agent tabletop pick-and-place. - **Problem:**
  1. **Unspecified opponent & `styleroll` trap:** Phase 2's gate (L391, L452) requires `gt_ns` to *"match or beat `gt1` in 96 paired TvZ games"* without naming the fixed Zerg opponent (`gz1` vs. `gz1dev`) while freezing Zerg work (L370). Any new `gt_ns` stochastic providers (`ConceptPerceptionProvider`, `BranchScorerProvider`, `TacticalOptionProvider`) must receive an explicit seeded RNG from `g.game_seed` in Phase 0 so paired comparisons and `eval/pairs.py` branches are genuinely independent across seeds.
